# Tutorial

In-app walkthrough shown from the menu ("How to play").

## Step 1: Share a code

Start a game and send the link (or code) to a friend. No accounts — the game lives on your two devices.

## Step 2: Set up in secret

Before playing, each of you arranges your own bishops and knights on the four open back-rank squares. Your opponent can’t see your choice.

## Step 3: The fanfare

When both of you confirm, the arrangements are revealed at the same time — then it’s chess as usual, White first.

## Step 4: Play at your pace

Moves sync live. You can offer a draw or resign from the game screen; the move list is in the panel at the bottom. Want to warm up first? Practice on your own from the games screen.

## Step 5: Say what you mean

The speech-bubble button opens a chat for that game. Tag a message with what you mean, mark the word you’d stress out loud, and react with an emoji.
