# Tutorial

In-app walkthrough. Access anytime via the menu ("How it works").

## Step 1: Pick where

Choose the whole world, a region like Africa, or a smaller part like Southern Africa. Every country and territory in it is included.

## Step 2: Pick what to name

Tick one or more: country names (from their flags), capitals, internet domains, dialing codes, currencies, how many official languages, or what the people are called.

## Step 3: Or just name them

Tick “Just name them” instead to name every place you can think of, in any order, with no clues. Type a name and press Enter or Add; it joins your list. A place already on your list isn’t added again, under any of its names. Your score is how many places your list names.

## Step 4: Fill in the boxes

Each country gets a box for each thing you picked. Type the answers you know. Nothing is marked while you play, and capitals and accents don’t matter.

## Step 5: Submit for your score

Your game is saved, so you can leave and come back. When you’re done, tap “Submit” to see how many you got right, then every answer marked right or wrong, with the right answer next to each one you missed.

## Step 6: Play a friend

Tap “Play a friend” instead and send the link. You both play the same game, see how far the other has got, and see both scores once you’ve both submitted. “Friends” in the bottom bar lists your games, and the chat button lets you talk while you play.

## Step 7: Names, alerts and ending

Give yourself a name so friends see it instead of “Your friend”: you’re asked the first time, and you can change it on the Friends screen. “Game alerts” in the menu is for being told when a friend joins or finishes; it’s new, so still check your games on the Friends screen. If a friend never finishes, “End the game” under your score ends it, and you win.

## Step 8: Keep it on your phone

Choose “Install app” in the menu to add Borderline to your home screen. It then opens like any other app, even without an internet connection.

## Step 9: Always up to date

When a new version comes out, the app downloads it and shows an Update button. If you don’t tap it, the new version installs itself the next time you open the app. To stop that, turn off “Automatic updates” in the menu.
