# User Guide

Borderline tests what you know about the countries of the world. You pick a
part of the world and what to name, then fill in every answer you know, on
your own or against a friend.

## Play a game

1. On the start screen, choose **Region**: the whole world, or one of Africa,
   Americas, Antarctic, Asia, Europe and Oceania. For a smaller game, choose a
   part of that region too, such as Southern Africa.
2. Under **What to name**, tick one or more topics: country names (from their
   flags), capitals, internet domains, dialing codes, currencies, how many
   official languages, and what the people are called. The line above the
   buttons says how many places and answers the game will have.
3. Tap **Play on your own**. Each country or territory gets a card, with a box
   for each topic. When you're naming countries, the card shows only the
   flag; otherwise it shows the flag and the name.
4. Type the answers you know. **Enter** (or **Next** on a phone keyboard)
   moves to the next box. Nothing is marked while you play: no box shows
   whether it's right, so change any answer as often as you like. The bar at
   the top counts how many boxes you've filled, and an orange line along its
   bottom edge grows as you fill them.

What counts as right when you submit:
- Capital letters, accents and punctuation don't matter: "st lucia" finds
  Saint Lucia.
- Other names for a country count too: "Côte d'Ivoire", or "cote divoire",
  finds Ivory Coast.
- For a country with several capitals, any one of them counts.
- For a currency, the name ("South African rand"), the short name ("rand") or
  the code ("ZAR") all count.
- For a dialing code, the "+" is optional.
- For official languages, type how many the country has, as a number ("3")
  or a word ("three"). It has to be the exact number.

Every country and territory in the area is included, even small ones. If the
data has no answer for something (Antarctica has no capital), that box isn't
shown and doesn't count.

## Just name them

A game with no clues: name every place you can think of in the area, in any
order.

1. Choose the region as usual, then tick **Just name them** at the top of
   **What to name**. The topics untick themselves: this game can't be mixed
   with them. Tick any topic to go back to a topic game.
2. Tap **Play on your own** (or **Play a friend**). There is one box. Type a
   name and press **Enter**, or tap **Add**: the name joins your list under
   the box, newest at the top. Tap **✕** beside a name to take it off.
3. Nothing on the list is marked right or wrong. A place that is already on
   your list isn't added again: the line under the box turns red and says so,
   and what you typed stays in the box so you can change it. That covers the
   same name written another way ("the Bahamas" and "bahamas") and another
   name for the same place ("Ivory Coast" when "Côte d'Ivoire" is on the
   list). The line under the box counts the names you've typed, not the right
   ones.
4. **Submit** ends the game: "You named 23 of 27." Each place counts once.
   The same typing rules apply as for country names (capitals, accents and
   other names for a place don't matter). What you type in the box but don't
   add isn't counted.

Your list is saved as you go, like any game, including a name half typed in
the box.

## Leave and come back

Your game on your own is saved on your device as you type, answers included. Go back to
the start screen, close the app, or turn off your phone: it will still be
there.

- On the start screen, **Continue** takes you back to it.
- On a phone, **Game** in the bottom bar does the same.
- Starting a different game on your own asks first, because it replaces the
  one you have. Starting a game with a friend doesn't touch it.

## Finish

Tap **Submit** in the bar at the top and confirm. The game ends and you see
your score in large numbers, for example 12/34, with "You got 12 of 34
right." under it. Under that, every place is listed with what you typed in
each box and a mark: **Right**, **Wrong** (what you typed is crossed out),
or **Blank** when you left it empty. Next to each one you missed is the
right answer. The answers can't be changed after that. **New game** takes you back to
the start screen.

In **Just name them**, the score is followed by your list with each name
marked **Right** or **Not counted** (not a place in this game, or misspelt),
and then the places you missed.

## Play a friend

You and a friend play the same game, each on your own phone, and compare
scores at the end. You need an internet connection for this; playing on your
own doesn't.

**Start a game**
1. On the start screen, choose where and what to name, as for a game on your
   own. **Just name them** works too: you both get the same area, and the
   live line counts the names your friend has typed ("Sam has typed 23
   names"), never how many are right.
2. Tap **Play a friend**. The first time, you're asked what your friend should
   call you: type a name (up to 20 characters) and tap **Save**, or tap
   **Skip** to stay "Your friend". The game then opens with a box at the top:
   **Send this to a friend**.
3. Tap **Share link** to send it with your phone's share sheet (or it's copied,
   ready to paste), or **Copy code** to send just the code, such as
   `happy-tiger`. The shared message says where and what, for example "Play
   me at Borderline: Southern Africa (Capital).", and in most messaging apps
   the link shows a card reading "A friend wants a game."
4. You can start typing straight away. You don't have to wait for your friend.

**Join a friend's game**
- Open the link they sent and tap **Join game**. The first time, you're asked
  for a name the same way.
- Or open **Friends** in the bottom bar, tap **Join with a code**, type the
  code and tap **Join game**.
- A code works once: after one friend has joined, nobody else can.

**While you play**
- The line under the title shows how your friend is doing: "Nobody has joined
  yet", "Sam has filled 12 of 34", or "Sam has finished" (with their name if
  they gave one, "Your friend" if not). It counts filled boxes only. It never
  says whether their answers are right.
- Your answers are saved on your phone as you type, like a game on your own.
  They never leave your phone: when you submit, only your score is sent.
- When you're done, tap **Submit** and confirm. You see your score and your
  answers marked straight away. Your friend sees your score only once they've
  submitted too.
- If nobody has joined yet when you submit, the link stays on screen so you
  can still send it.

**The result**
- When you've both submitted, you both see who won and both scores, for
  example "You won! You 12 of 34, Sam 9 of 34". Equal scores are a tie.
  Under the result you each see your own answers marked, as in a game on your
  own. Your friend's answers never reach your phone, so you can't see theirs.
- **Play again** starts a new game with the same friend, the same places and
  the same topics. If your friend taps it first, you see **Go to the next
  game** instead.

**End a game your friend doesn't finish**
- Once you've submitted and your friend has joined but not submitted, **End
  the game** appears under your score. Confirm it and the game is over: you
  win, and your friend's score shows as "Didn't finish".
- Your friend sees "Sam ended the game before you finished". Their boxes go
  away and what they typed can't be sent any more.

**Cancel a game**
- While nobody has joined, **Cancel this game** under the link ends it. The
  link stops working and what you typed is deleted.

**All your games with friends**
- **Friends** in the bottom bar lists them, newest first. Each says who it's
  with ("with Sam", or "Waiting for a friend to join") and what it needs from
  you: **Your turn** (you haven't submitted), **Their turn**, **You won**,
  **You lost**, **A tie** or **Cancelled**.
- You can have several going at once, with different friends. Your game on
  your own is separate and isn't affected.

**Your name**
- The top of the **Friends** screen says how your friends see you. Tap
  **Change** (or **Add a name**) to set it. It changes in all your games at
  once. Save it empty to go back to "Your friend".

**When Borderline asks you to reload**
- "This game needs the newest version of Borderline. Reload to play it."
  means your friend set the game up with a newer version than the one on
  your phone. Tap **Reload** to get it; the game then opens as usual.
- "Borderline's server doesn't recognise this device any more" means the
  server has forgotten this phone, which can happen after a long time away.
  Tap **Reload** to keep playing with friends. Your earlier games with
  friends won't be in your list after that; your game on your own isn't
  affected.
- If it says to check your internet connection instead, nothing is lost:
  connect and try again.

**Game alerts**
- Choose **Game alerts** in the menu to turn them on. Your phone asks whether
  Borderline may show notifications: allow it.
- They are meant to tell you when a friend joins your game, when they finish
  (so you can see who won), and when they end a game before you finished, and
  a tap on one is meant to open that game. That part hasn't been checked on a
  real phone yet, so don't rely on it until it has.
- They're off until you turn them on, and **Game alerts** again turns them
  off. If the menu says **Blocked**, notifications are turned off for this
  site in your browser's settings; turn them on there first.

## Chat with your friend

Once your friend has joined, a chat button appears: bottom right on a phone,
top right on a computer. A dot on it means your friend has written something
you haven't seen. The chat's title shows your friend's name if they gave one.

- Type a message and tap the send arrow (or press Enter on a keyboard).
- Tap the tag button beside the box to add up to two tags, such as
  **[joking]** or **[serious]**, or your own word. The same tray lets you tap
  up to two words to stress, the way you'd say them out loud; they show in
  italics with a stroke underneath.
- Tap **+** under a message to react with 👍 ❤️ 😂 😮 😢 or 🙏. Tap your
  reaction again to take it back.
- Close the chat with ✕, by swiping it to the right, with Escape, or with your
  phone's back button. Each game has its own chat, and a new game after
  **Play again** starts a new one.

## Open the menu

- **On a phone:** tap the three-line button at the bottom left.
- **On a computer:** click the three-line button at the top right.
- **To close it:** tap outside it, tap the ✕, press Escape, swipe it back to
  the left, or use your phone's back button.

## See how it works

Choose **How it works** in the menu. A short guide opens. Close it with **Got it**, by tapping outside it, or with the
back button.

## Switch between light and dark

Choose **Dark mode** or **Light mode** in the menu. The screen changes straight
away and the app remembers your choice. Until you choose, it follows your
device's own setting.

## Install the app

Choose **Install app** in the menu. The item only appears when your browser can
install apps and the app is not installed yet.

- Some browsers show their own install window. Confirm it.
- Others show step-by-step instructions for that browser. Follow them.
- On iPhone and iPad, only Safari can install web apps. The instructions say
  so if you are in another browser.
- **Not now** hides the install item for good on that device.

Once installed, Borderline opens from your home screen or dock like any other
app, and it opens without an internet connection.

## Updates

When a new version comes out, the app downloads it in the background and shows
a bar at the bottom: **A new version is available.**

- **Update** switches to the new version straight away.
- **Later** keeps the current version for now. With **Automatic updates** on
  (the default), the new version then installs itself the next time you open
  the app.
- With **Automatic updates** off in the menu, nothing installs by itself; the
  bar comes back each time you open the app until you tap **Update**.
- **Check for updates** in the menu looks for a new version right away and says
  what it found.
