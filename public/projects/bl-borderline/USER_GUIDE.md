# User Guide

Borderline tests what you know about the countries of the world. You pick a
part of the world and what to name, then fill in every answer you know.

## Play a game

1. On the start screen, choose **Region**: the whole world, or one of Africa,
   Americas, Antarctic, Asia, Europe and Oceania. For a smaller game, choose a
   part of that region too, such as Southern Africa.
2. Under **What to name**, tick one or more topics: country names (from their
   flags), capitals, internet domains, dialing codes, currencies, languages,
   and what the people are called. The line above **Start** says how many
   places and answers the game will have.
3. Tap **Start**. Each country or territory gets a card, with a box for each
   topic. When you're naming countries, the card shows only the flag;
   otherwise it shows the flag and the name.
4. Type into a box. As soon as your answer is right, the box turns green and
   the next empty box is selected, so you can keep typing. A wrong answer
   stays in its box; nothing tells you it's wrong, so you can fix it or move
   on.

What counts as right:
- Capital letters, accents and punctuation don't matter: "st lucia" finds
  Saint Lucia.
- Other names for a country count too: "Côte d'Ivoire", or "cote divoire",
  finds Ivory Coast.
- For a country with several capitals, any one of them counts.
- For a currency, the name ("South African rand"), the short name ("rand") or
  the code ("ZAR") all count.
- For a dialing code, the "+" is optional.
- For a language, any official language of that country counts.

Every country and territory in the area is included, even small ones. If the
data has no answer for something (Antarctica has no capital), that box isn't
shown and doesn't count.

## Leave and come back

Your game is saved on your device as you play. Go back to the start screen,
close the app, or turn off your phone: it will still be there.

- On the start screen, **Continue** takes you back to it.
- On a phone, **Game** in the bottom bar does the same.
- Starting a different game asks first, because it replaces the one you have.

## Finish

The game ends when every box is green, or when you tap **Give up** at the
bottom of the list and confirm. You then see your score, for example "You found
12 of 34." The answers you missed are not shown. **New game** takes you back to
the start screen.

## Open the menu

- **On a phone:** tap the three-line button at the bottom left.
- **On a computer:** click the three-line button at the top right.
- **To close it:** tap outside it, tap the ✕, press Escape, swipe it back to
  the left, or use your phone's back button.

## See how it works

Choose **How it works** in the menu. A short guide opens. Close it with **Got it**, by tapping outside it, or with the
back button.

## Switch between light and dark

Choose **Dark mode** or **Light mode** in the menu. The screen changes straight
away and the app remembers your choice. Until you choose, it follows your
device's own setting.

## Install the app

Choose **Install app** in the menu. The item only appears when your browser can
install apps and the app is not installed yet.

- Some browsers show their own install window. Confirm it.
- Others show step-by-step instructions for that browser. Follow them.
- On iPhone and iPad, only Safari can install web apps. The instructions say
  so if you are in another browser.
- **Not now** hides the install item for good on that device.

Once installed, Borderline opens from your home screen or dock like any other
app, and it opens without an internet connection.

## Updates

When a new version comes out, the app downloads it in the background and shows
a bar at the bottom: **A new version is available.**

- **Update** switches to the new version straight away.
- **Later** keeps the current version for now. With **Automatic updates** on
  (the default), the new version then installs itself the next time you open
  the app.
- With **Automatic updates** off in the menu, nothing installs by itself; the
  bar comes back each time you open the app until you tap **Update**.
- **Check for updates** in the menu looks for a new version right away and says
  what it found.
