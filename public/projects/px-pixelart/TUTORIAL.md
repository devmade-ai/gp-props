# Tutorial

In-app walkthrough. Access anytime via the menu ("How it works").

## Step 1: Choose a size

On the New screen, type how many rows and columns you want, from 1 to 64 each, then tap “Create grid”. Every square starts white.

## Step 2: Colour a square

Tap a square to open the colour window. Pick a colour, type a colour code like #FF8800, or tap one of the colours already in your picture. Then tap “Colour”.

## Step 3: Colour many at once

In the colour window, tap more squares in the grid to pick them too, or tap “Pick all … squares” to pick every square that has the same colour as the one you tapped. Every picked square gets the new colour.

## Step 4: Copy and paste colours

“Copy” copies the colour code. Open another square and tap “Paste” to use the same colour there.

## Step 5: Save your picture

“Save” keeps your picture under Saved. “Save as new” keeps a copy under a new name and leaves the one you started from as it was.

## Step 6: Open or delete

Tap “Saved” to see your saved pictures. “Open” carries on drawing one; “Delete” removes it for good. The app asks first before anything is lost.

## Step 7: Keep it on your phone

Choose “Install app” in the menu to add Pixel Art to your home screen. It then opens like any other app, even without an internet connection.

## Step 8: Always up to date

When a new version comes out, the app downloads it and shows an Update button. If you don’t tap it, the new version installs itself the next time you open the app. To stop that, turn off “Automatic updates” in the menu.
