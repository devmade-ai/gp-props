# User Guide

Pixel Art makes pixel pictures. You choose how many rows and columns of
squares you want, then colour the squares.

## Start a picture

1. On the **New** screen, type how many **Rows** and **Columns** you want.
   Each can be from 1 to 64. Both start at 16.
2. Tap **Create grid**. The picture opens on the **Draw** screen, with every
   square white, or black if the app is in dark mode. The squares keep that
   colour if you switch modes later.

If a number is outside 1 to 64, or isn't a number, the box says so and nothing
is created.

If the picture you were drawing has changes that aren't saved, the app asks
before starting a new one: **Start new picture** throws those changes away,
**Keep drawing it** goes back. A blank or saved picture is replaced without
asking, because nothing is lost.

The New screen also shows the picture you're drawing, with **Continue** to go
back to it.

## Colour squares

1. On the **Draw** screen, tap a square. The colour window opens.
2. Choose a colour in any of these ways:
   - Tap the big colour bar under **Pick a colour** to use your device's
     colour picker.
   - Type a colour code in **Colour code**, such as `#FF8800`. Codes have a
     `#` and six letters or numbers (0 to 9 and A to F); the short form
     `#F80` works too.
   - Tap one of the samples under **Colours in this picture** to reuse a
     colour you've already used. The picked one has a ring round it.
3. Tap **Colour 1 square**. The window closes and the square changes.

**Cancel**, the ✕, tapping outside the window, Escape and the phone's Back
button all close the window without changing anything.

### Colour many squares at once

In the colour window, under **Squares to colour**, the picture is shown again.
The square you tapped is already picked.

- Tap more squares to pick them. Tap a picked square again to unpick it.
- **Pick all #RRGGBB squares** picks every square that has the same colour as
  the one you tapped. For example, on a new picture it picks every blank
  square, which fills the background.
- **Pick all** picks every square; **Unpick all** clears the picks.

Picked squares have a ring and show the new colour, so you can see the result
before you apply it. The button at the bottom says how many squares it will
colour, such as **Colour 12 squares**.

### Insert a row or column

In the colour window, under **Rows and columns**:
- **Insert row above** and **Insert row below** add a blank row next to the
  square you tapped.
- **Insert column left** and **Insert column right** add a blank column next
  to it.

The new squares are white, or black if the app is in dark mode. The window
closes and the picture grows by one row or column; the other squares keep
their colours. A picture can have up to 64 rows and 64 columns; once it has
64, those buttons are off and the window says why.

### Copy and paste a colour

- **Copy** copies the colour code in the box, such as `#FF8800`.
- **Paste** puts a copied colour code into the box. It also finds the code in
  text copied from other apps, such as `color: #ff8800;`.

To give one square the same colour as another: tap the first square, tap
**Copy**, close the window, tap the second square, tap **Paste**, then tap
**Colour 1 square**.

If what you copied isn't a colour code, the app says so and keeps the colour
you had. Some browsers don't let apps read what you copied; the app then
tells you to press and hold the **Colour code** box and choose **Paste**.

## Save your picture

At the top of the Draw screen are the picture's name, its size and whether
it's saved: **Not saved yet**, **Saved**, or **Changes not saved**.

- **Save** keeps the picture. The first time, it asks for a name; the box
  suggests one, such as "Picture 1". After that, **Save** updates the saved
  picture without asking.
- **Save as new** always asks for a name and keeps a separate copy. The
  picture you started from stays as it was. The name box suggests the old
  name with "copy" after it.

If the device's storage for the app is full, the app says the picture wasn't
saved. Delete a saved picture you no longer need, then save again.

The picture you're drawing is also kept on the device as you work, so leaving
the app, reloading or closing it never loses squares. Saving is what puts it
on the **Saved** list.

## Open or delete a saved picture

Tap **Saved**. Each saved picture shows a small preview, its name, its size
and when it was saved. The one you're drawing is marked **Open now**.

- **Open** carries on drawing that picture. If the picture you were drawing
  has changes that aren't saved, the app asks first, because opening another
  picture throws those changes away.
- **Delete** removes the saved picture for good, after you confirm. If it's
  the picture you're drawing, it stays open on the Draw screen as a picture
  that isn't saved; **Save** then asks for a name again.

## Use the keyboard

On the Draw screen and in the colour window, the picture is one stop for the
Tab key. The arrow keys move between squares, Home and End go to the start
and end of a row, and Ctrl+Home / Ctrl+End go to the first and last square.
Enter or Space opens the colour window (on the Draw screen) or picks and
unpicks the square (in the colour window).

## Menu

The menu button is at the bottom left on a phone, and at the top right on a
computer.

- **How it works**: a short guide to the app.
- **Dark mode** / **Light mode**: switches the look. The app follows your
  device's setting until you choose.
- **Automatic updates**: on by default. New versions install themselves the
  next time the app opens. Turn it off to update only when you tap **Update**.
- **Check for updates**: looks for a new version now.
- **Install app**: adds Pixel Art to your home screen or dock, when your
  browser supports it. Some browsers show step-by-step instructions instead.
